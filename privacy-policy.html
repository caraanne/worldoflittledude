Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At Big World of Little Dude, your privacy matters. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and how we keep it safe when you visit worldoflittledude.com or purchase from our shop.

1. Who We Are

Big World of Little Dude is owned and operated by Cara Zelas. If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us at hello@worldoflittledude.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Email signups: When you sign up for our free Activity Pack or newsletter, we collect your name and email address.
  • Purchases: When you buy through our shop (powered by Shopify), we collect your name, email, shipping address and payment details. Payment information is processed securely by Shopify and is never stored by us directly.
  • Contact form: When you submit a message through our contact page, we collect your name, email address and message content.
  • Analytics: We use tools such as Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This may include your browser type, pages visited and general location. No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver your free Activity Pack and any products you purchase
  • To send you emails you signed up for, including our newsletter and updates
  • To respond to messages you send us through our contact form
  • To improve our website and understand what content is helpful to our community

4. Email Marketing

When you sign up for our free resource or newsletter, you are added to our email list managed through Systeme.io. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email we send.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share information only with the third-party services that help us run our business:

  • Shopify for processing purchases
  • Systeme.io for email marketing
  • Google Analytics for website analytics

Each of these services has their own privacy policy and handles your data according to their terms.

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to help us understand how visitors use our site (via Google Analytics). You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Shopify also uses cookies when you make a purchase to manage your cart and checkout experience.

7. Children's Privacy

Our products are designed for children, but this website is intended for parents, educators and caregivers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at hello@worldoflittledude.com and we will promptly delete it.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. To make a request, please email us at hello@worldoflittledude.com.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. All purchases are processed through Shopify's secure, encrypted checkout. Email data is stored securely through Systeme.io.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to check back periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@worldoflittledude.com.
