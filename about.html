About

Hi, I'm Cara.
And this is Little Dude.

Not everything worth teaching fits on a report card.

How to make a friend. What to do with a big feeling. How to keep trying when something feels hard. How to be kind.

These are skills, too. And just like ABCs and 123s, we can teach them.

A little about me

I'm Cara Zelas.

Early childhood educator, Montessori teacher, children's author and the creator of Big World of Little Dude.

I've spent my career working with young children in classrooms and communities, and I've seen the same thing again and again:

Little humans are expected to know how to share, listen, make friends, manage their feelings, respect someone's space and bounce back when things don't go their way.

But they're still learning how.

I created Big World of Little Dude to make those conversations easier. For children and the grown-ups helping them navigate their big world.

Through stories, characters and simple activities, Little Dude gives children a concrete way to explore some of life's biggest skills.

And the whole thing started with a very little dog.

Meet Little Dude

A real dog with some very big lessons to share.

Little Dude isn't just a character in a book. He's a real dog.

Little Dude and I became a certified therapy dog team and began volunteering together in hospitals, schools and classrooms. And I noticed something. Children opened up around him.

Little lessons from a little dog

  • A conversation about personal space could become a conversation about giving a dog space.
  • Gentle hands could become a lesson about respect.
  • Waiting for Little Dude could teach patience.
  • Noticing when he wanted to move away could start a conversation about boundaries and consent.

Big ideas suddenly became concrete, playful and much easier for little humans to understand. That was the spark behind Big World of Little Dude.

Nine stories. Nine important skills.

Today, Little Dude helps children explore kindness, feelings, friendship and so much more — one adventure at a time.
